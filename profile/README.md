# OS2DLLS - Libraries and DLLs for OS/2

**Preserving software. Sharing libraries. Keeping OS/2 alive.**

Welcome to **OS2DLLS**, a collection of open-source projects, libraries, DLLs, and development resources related to IBM OS/2 and compatible operating systems.

## A Note About My Forks

To avoid any misunderstandings, I would like to clarify the purpose of the forks listed here.

These forks are **not intended to replace the original repositories**. They are neither an attempt to pursue parallel development nor an effort to create competing projects.

The work done by Paul, BWW, and KO is invaluable, and the entire OS/2 community owes them a great deal. I want to make it clear that my forks are in no way intended to diminish or undermine their contributions.

These repositories contain modifications and bug fixes required specifically for my cross-compilation environment. I cannot fully assess the impact of these changes on other environments or use cases, but they are important for building and distributing my own software, particularly **Vacuum**.

So, if you are looking for GCC for OS/2, please use the **original project**, not my fork.

### Why publish these forks at all?

The answer is simple: **transparency and knowledge sharing**.

The OS/2 community is small and, unfortunately, continues to shrink. We cannot afford to keep knowledge and experience to ourselves. Quite the opposite: we need to share what we learn so that others can benefit from it and build upon it.

That is why I intend to document in detail how I compiled each component and which modifications were necessary. This information will be available in each respective repository.

The goal is to make it clear what I changed, why those changes were necessary, and how the results can be reproduced.

My intention is not to replace existing projects, but to make my work transparent and contribute, in my own small way, to the OS/2 community.
